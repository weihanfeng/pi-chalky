# Doodles
The user draws on the page and clicks through it, then sends it with a few words: "[doodles on the UI]", with a
picture for each step.
- Read the steps in order, as a story; look closely at every picture: the drawing is the message.
- If it could mean two quite different things, propose one in a line and ask; otherwise do it.
Before you handle a doodle, read README.md.
